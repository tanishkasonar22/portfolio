06

Buy to Door

Buy to Door is an age-verified ticket shop built on the Credence Cloud SDK. A buyer proves they are over 21 from the digital ID in their phone wallet, and the shop learns only one thing: yes or no. It stores no name, birth date or photo.

A strict rule in the shop, not an AI model, makes the final decision. Only a clear “yes” passes, and the buyer gets a signed, single-use ticket and a privacy receipt. Buyers without a digital ID can still buy a ticket marked for an ID check at the gate. Organisers get a dashboard, a setup-readiness check and a gate view, none of which show personal details.

It works with a simulated wallet (mock mode) and with a real Android wallet answering live Credence requests. It has 266 automated tests.

Credence Cloud SDK Digital ID Age verification Privacy Android wallet
